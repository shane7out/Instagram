Good questions

Everything people ask before their first trip.

When is the best time to visit Thailand?

November to February is cool season — dry, sunny, 24–32°C nearly everywhere. The Andaman coast is best November–April; the Gulf islands stay dry into September. We build your route so every stop lands in its best weather window.

How much does a tailor-made trip cost?

Most travelers spend $180–$320 per person per day, including boutique hotels, private guides, internal travel and most meals. Your quote is priced line-by-line, so you always see where every dollar goes.

Is the itinerary draft really free?

Yes. You get a full day-by-day draft within 48 hours, with no obligation and no payment details required. You only pay when you decide to book — and the draft is yours to keep either way.

What if our plans change?

Changes are free until 30 days before travel. Inside 30 days we'll still move everything we can — and while you're in Thailand, our Bangkok team answers 24/7 to reroute you around weather, strikes or spontaneous changes of heart.

Do you handle flights, visas and insurance?

We book all internal flights, trains and transfers. For international flights we advise but you book direct (it protects your consumer rights). Most nationalities get 60 days visa-free; we'll confirm the rules for your passport in your first draft.
