Good to know

Questions buyers ask us

Are the gold bars and coins genuine and certified?

Yes. We source only from LBMA Good Delivery refiners and sovereign mints. Bars ship with assay certification, and every item is verified on arrival at our depository before it reaches you.

What premium am I paying over spot?

Each product page shows the live spot price plus a transparent premium that covers minting, fabrication, and handling. Premiums are lowest on larger bars and highest on small fractional coins.

Can I take physical delivery of vaulted metal?

Always. Because your holdings are fully allocated, you can request delivery of your exact bars and coins at any time, or sell them back to us at the live bid price.

Is my purchase reported or taxed?

Reporting requirements depend on your jurisdiction and the product. We provide clear documentation for every order; for tax specifics we recommend speaking with your own advisor.

How is shipping protected?

All deliveries are fully insured, require an adult signature, and arrive in discreet, unmarked, tamper-evident packaging. You are covered for 100% of value until the parcel is in your hands.
