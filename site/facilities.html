Facilities

The house and the grounds

Fifteen rooms, shared spaces that suit both silence and conversation, and a hillside made for walking.

Guest house

Fifteen rooms, up to thirty retreatants

Each room holds two single beds with a desk and chair, and opens onto either a balcony or a small patio. Every room has a private bath with a walk-in shower. Two rooms are equipped for accessibility.

Booked as singles, the house suits a group of fifteen who each want their own door to close. Shared, it accommodates thirty. Groups usually decide this with us during planning.

  • Two single beds, desk, and chair in every room
  • Private bath with walk-in shower
  • Balcony or patio off each room
  • Two rooms equipped for accessibility
  • Linens and towels provided

Shared spaces

Rooms for the whole group

Chapel

Open through the day and evening for prayer, liturgy, and silence. Groups are welcome to use it for their own scheduled services; individual guests find it quiet at almost every hour.

Library

A settled room lined with books, with chairs by the windows. Reliably the quietest space in the house and the one most guests end up claiming for an afternoon.

Lounge

Comfortable seating for conversation, small-group sessions, or an unhurried cup of coffee. It carries the social side of a retreat so the rest of the house can stay quiet.

Dining room

Seating for the full house at shared tables. Meals are arranged in advance with your group, and dietary needs can be accommodated with notice.

Meeting space

Room to present, discuss, and break out again. Tell us the format of your sessions during planning and we will set the space to match.

Spiritual direction

Available on request for guests on personal retreat and for groups who would like it offered during their stay. Please ask when you inquire so we can arrange it.

Outdoors

The grounds

The hillside is part of the retreat, not the scenery around it. Paths run from the guest house through the gardens and out into the oaks, with benches set where the valley opens below.

  • A granite-lined grotto
  • Stations of the Cross along the hillside
  • A walking labyrinth
  • Nature trails through oak and madrone
  • Gardens, benches, and open views over the Rogue River Valley
