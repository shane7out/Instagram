Availability & Booking

Start with an inquiry

Send your preferred dates, your group size, and what you hope the retreat will hold. We review every inquiry and reply within one to two business days.

How booking works

Three steps

  1. Send your inquiry

    Give us a date range rather than a single date if you can — it widens what we are able to offer you, especially in spring and autumn.

  2. We confirm availability

    Within one to two business days we will write back with what is open, what the house can hold for your group, and the next steps.

  3. Contract and deposit

    Your dates are held once the contract is signed and the deposit received. The contract sets out meals, capacity, and check-in and check-out times.

Inquiry form

Request your dates

We reply to every inquiry within one to two business days. Sending this form does not hold a date; dates are held on a signed contract and deposit.

Good to know

Deposits, capacity, and cancellation

Holding your dates

Dates are confirmed once we have a signed contract and your deposit. Until then an inquiry is a conversation, not a reservation — so if your plans are firm, it is worth moving early.

Cancellation

Cancelling more than 90 days before your event refunds 50% of the deposit. Cancelling within 90 days of the event forfeits the deposit.

Capacity

Capacity depends on the type of event and how rooms are shared. Your contract states the maximum number of overnight guests and commuters for your retreat.

Check-in and check-out

Times are set in your contract so that the house can be turned over between groups. Let us know in advance if your travel makes those times difficult.
