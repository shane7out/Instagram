FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SparkyLoan a lender?

No. SparkyLoan is not a lender. We operate as a matching service that connects consumers with a network of lenders who may be able to help.

How much can I request?

You can request up to $35,000. The amount you're ultimately offered depends on the lender you're matched with and your individual circumstances.

Will this affect my credit score?

Submitting a request through our platform does not directly impact your credit score. However, if you're connected with a lender and proceed with an application, that lender may perform a credit check which could affect your score.

How fast can I get funds?

Once you're approved by a lender, funds may be deposited directly into your bank account as soon as the next business day.

How much does it cost to use SparkyLoan?

It's completely free to submit a loan request. We never charge a fee to match you with lenders in our network.

Do you accept all credit types?

Yes. Our diverse lender network considers applicants with all types of credit histories, so you're welcome to submit a request regardless of your credit.
