Finding us

Off Blackwell Road, above the valley

We are a short drive from Interstate 5 between Gold Hill and Central Point, with Medford, Jacksonville, and Ashland all close by. The drive up is signed from Blackwell Road.

Medford Airport is roughly twenty-five minutes away. Groups arriving together are welcome to ask about parking when they book.
