## Plain pitch (draft)
"Restaurant Rewards (RR) are a simple way for guests to pay for food and tip at participating restaurants. 1 RR = $1 of credit at a participating restaurant. Restaurants choose to take part, and can switch it on or off any time. Want your restaurant listed? Contact keytechnologies.si."

## Questions the attorney must answer first
1. Is a restaurant giving us dollars for RR the sale of stored value / a gift certificate / prepaid access? Registration or licensing in Nevada or federally?

## UPDATE (Shane, same day): THE MODEL IS BARTER, NO MONEY
"I exchange. There's no money exchanged. I repost on the stories page of Las Vegas Restaurants. In exchange they give me credit, and I give that credit to the audience. There's never money exchanged. I don't want to ever get in trouble."
- Restaurant gives credit (food credit) -> Key Technologies / Las Vegas Restaurants reposts the restaurant on its stories (promotion) -> the credit is given to the audience as RR.
- No payment by the restaurant, no payment by guests, nothing sold. This is much closer to a promotional/barter arrangement than to selling stored value.
- The card back now says (live): "No money is exchanged. A restaurant takes part by giving credit in exchange for a promotion (a repost on the Las Vegas Restaurants stories), and we give that credit to the community as Restaurant Rewards."
- "1 RR = $1 USD" is STILL NOT on any page. If each restaurant's credit is stated in dollars, the restaurant (not us) honors its own credit; wording must be reviewed.
Attorney/accountant questions for the barter model: (1) tax treatment of promotion-for-credit for both sides (IRS barter income); (2) does handing restaurant-issued credit to the public make us a gift-card/loyalty issuer or a promoter, and who is responsible when a restaurant stops honoring it; (3) a short written agreement per restaurant (what credit, how long, who can stop it, no money); (4) tipping with RR; (5) disclosure wording for the stories reposts (paid/promotional relationship disclosure rules for influencers).
