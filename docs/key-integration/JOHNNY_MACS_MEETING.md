# Johnny Mac's owner meeting (Thursday)

Open on a phone or tablet: https://keytechnologies.si/johnny-macs-demo/wallet.html  (4 big steps, one button each; unlisted)
Fuller demo with menu + map: https://keytechnologies.si/johnny-macs-demo/

## What the wallet demo shows (his words, kept simple)
1. Johnny Mac's has a wallet. We put 500 Restaurant Rewards in it.
2. A customer (Alex) eats there and earns 15 rewards from it (wallet 500 -> 485).
3. Alex taps Redeem for free Mozzarella Sticks and gets a code to show the server. The 15 rewards go back into Johnny Mac's wallet (485 -> 500).
4. Nothing to install, he can see every reward used, customers come back.

## Say it in one breath
"Your customers earn rewards when they eat here, they use them here, and every reward they use goes back in your wallet. You can see all of it."

## Decide BEFORE the meeting (the demo shows the simplest version; the real rules must be written down)
- Who funds the 500 rewards, and is it paid for? (the LVR system already has a funding ledger in dollars; the demo shows no money on purpose)
- When a customer redeems, do the rewards return to the restaurant's pool (as shown) or are they settled some other way?
- Rewards stay a free perk with no cash value, never money or property; the wording in the Program Terms must stay true for what you promise him.
- Do not promise sending rewards between people (not allowed by the terms).

## For a man in his 70s
- Show it on a phone or tablet held in his hands; let him tap Next himself.
- Say the four steps out loud; do not show the dashboard, the map or the menu unless he asks.
- Leave him one thing: the link on a card or text, and your number.
