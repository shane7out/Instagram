Solutions

Problem first. Technology second.

Key Technologies doesn't start by selling AI. It starts by understanding what's actually slowing a business down, then figures out whether AI, automation, or a simpler fix solves it.

"We're losing leads."

Often a sales and lead-management problem before it's an AI problem — the fix might be better follow-up systems, not a new tool.

"Our team repeats the same work."

A business-automation problem. The goal is connecting the steps that already exist, not adding more software on top.

"We can't find our own information."

A data and knowledge-systems problem — information exists somewhere in the business, it's just not organized in a way people can use.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Key Technologies do?

Key Technologies helps businesses identify where AI, automation and better business systems can actually improve how they operate — then builds the systems to do it.

How can AI help my business?

It depends on the business. AI can help with sales and lead follow-up, customer communication, repetitive operational work, organizing information, and more — the point of a Key Technologies review is figuring out which of those actually apply to you.

Do I need to know which AI tools I need?

No. Most businesses don't, and that's the point of starting with the business problem instead of a specific tool. Key Technologies figures out what fits.

Can Key Technologies help small businesses?

Yes. Small businesses are often where the clearest, fastest wins are — less existing complexity to work around.

Can Key Technologies work with larger companies?

Yes. Larger organizations typically have more existing systems to map and integrate with, which the Key Technologies Method is built to handle.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that can carry out a defined task — like qualifying a lead, answering a common customer question, or pulling information together — rather than just responding to a single prompt.

What business processes can be automated?

Commonly: lead intake and follow-up, repetitive data entry, routine customer responses, internal reporting, and connecting tools that currently require manual work between them.

Can Key Technologies work with my existing software?

Usually, yes. The goal is to work with the systems a business already has rather than replace everything.

Can Key Technologies help with databases?

Yes — organizing, structuring and making business data easier to find and use is one of Key Technologies' core areas.

Can Key Technologies help with security and networking?

Yes. Shane's background includes high-end computer security and networking, and that's part of how AI and automation get evaluated — not bolted on without regard for it.

How does an AI consultation work?

It starts with understanding your business (the MAP step), then identifying and prioritizing real opportunities — see "How It Works" above for the full process.

What happens after I submit Explore My Options?

Shane or the Key Technologies team follows up directly to learn more about your business and talk through whether — and where — AI makes sense for you.
