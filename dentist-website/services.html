Our services

Everything your smile needs

Preventive, cosmetic, restorative, and specialty care — delivered by one attentive team, with technology that makes every visit faster and gentler.

Home / Services

Preventive & Hygiene

Comprehensive exams, digital X-rays, professional cleanings, fluoride, and sealants. We catch issues early — the gentlest, most affordable dentistry there is.

Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening brightens your smile up to 8 shades in about an hour, plus custom take-home trays for touch-ups whenever you like.

Veneers & Bonding

Hand-crafted porcelain veneers and same-day composite bonding to reshape, close gaps, and perfect your smile — natural-looking, never bulky.

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth without metal braces. We map your full treatment in 3D so you can preview your new smile before you ever start.

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth permanently. Guided implant surgery and lifelike crowns restore full chewing strength — built to last decades.

Crowns, Bridges & Root Canals

Restorative care that saves natural teeth — tooth-colored crowns, bridges, and comfortable, modern root canal therapy.

Family & Pediatric

Gentle checkups, cleanings, and cavity prevention for kids and teens in a friendly, low-stress environment they'll actually enjoy.

Emergency Dentistry

Same-day relief for toothaches, chips, and lost fillings. Call before noon and we'll do everything we can to see you the same day.

Sedation & Comfort

Nitrous oxide and oral sedation options for anxious patients, so even longer treatments feel calm and completely manageable.

Modern by design

Technology that makes dentistry gentler

Faster diagnoses, fewer appointments, and far more comfortable visits — powered by tools that put you at ease.

3D Digital Scanning

No more goopy impressions — a quick, comfortable digital scan captures your smile in minutes.

Same-Day Crowns

In-house milling means many crowns are designed, made, and placed in a single visit.

Low-Dose Digital X-Ray

Up to 90% less radiation than film, with instant, ultra-clear images you can see on screen.

Soft-Tissue Laser

Precise, minimally invasive gum treatment that heals faster with less discomfort.
