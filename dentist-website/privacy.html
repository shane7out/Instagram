Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Lumina Dental Studio ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. This is sample content for a demo website and is not legal advice.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — your name, contact details, and any notes you provide when you request an appointment or contact us.
  • Health information — clinical and treatment records collected as part of providing dental care, handled in accordance with applicable health-privacy laws.
  • Automatic information — basic technical data such as your device type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and improve it.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and provide your dental care.
  • To respond to your questions and appointment requests.
  • To send you information you've asked for, such as reminders or our newsletter (you can opt out anytime).
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, and insurance obligations.

How we protect it

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. Access to health records is limited to staff who need it to provide your care. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with your insurance provider to process claims, with trusted service providers who help us operate, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Request access to, or correction of, your personal information.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message.
  • Ask questions about how your data is handled.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@luminadental.com or call (555) 123-4567. You can also write to us at 128 Harborview Ave, Suite 200, Seattle, WA 98101.
