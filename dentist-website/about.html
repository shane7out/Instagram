Where it began

A different kind of dental office

Dr. Elena Vance opened Lumina after years of seeing patients rush through impersonal, high-volume practices. She wanted to build somewhere different — a place where you're greeted by name, never upsold, and never made to feel rushed.

Today our team of six clinicians shares that same standard: listen first, explain everything, and treat every patient the way we'd treat our own family. It's dentistry with the volume turned down and the care turned all the way up.
