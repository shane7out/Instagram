Before you ask

Do I need a fancy kitchen?

No. Our chefs have cooked tasting menus off four burners and a folding table. If your kitchen boils water, we can work with it — and anything it can't do, the chef brings equipment for.

What does it actually cost?

Per-guest pricing starts at the numbers on each experience above and moves with your menu — wagyu and caviar cost more than chicken thighs, as they should. You approve an exact all-in quote before anything is booked.

Groceries, drinks, gratuity — included?

Ingredients, service, and cleanup are always included in the quote. Alcohol is yours to provide (we'll tell you exactly what to buy for pairings). Gratuity is never required and always appreciated.

How far ahead should I book?

A week ahead is comfortable. Weekends and holidays go first — for those, two to three weeks is wise. Last-minute? Ask anyway; chefs occasionally have open evenings.

Can the chef handle dietary restrictions?

Yes — allergies, keto, kosher-style, vegan, gluten-free, or simply "no cilantro, ever." It all goes into the menu consult, and the menu is built around it rather than worked around it.
