AI terms in plain English: a glossary for business owners

Key Technologies · Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: here are the AI words business owners hear most, each in one or two plain sentences. Skim for the one you just heard, or read it top to bottom. If a term is not here, ask us on a free call.
AI (artificial intelligence)
Software that can do tasks that normally need human judgment, such as writing, summarizing, answering questions, sorting messages or understanding speech.
AI/SI
Artificial Intelligence / Super Intelligence. Key Technologies uses it as its name for putting the most advanced AI available to work as a practical business tool. It is not a claim that superintelligence exists. See What is AI/SI?
Superintelligence
A hypothetical AI that is better than the best humans at almost every thinking task. It does not exist today.
AGI (artificial general intelligence)
A hypothetical AI as capable as a person across most tasks. It is a research goal, not a product you can buy today.
LLM (large language model)
The kind of AI behind many chatbots. It is trained on large amounts of text and predicts useful words, which is why it can write, summarize and answer questions. It can also be wrong.
Chatbot
A program you talk to by typing. Some are simple scripts; others use an LLM.
AI assistant
An AI tool set up for a specific job, such as answering your business phone or drafting replies to reviews, usually with limits on what it may do.
AI agent
An AI assistant that can take steps on its own, such as looking up information or filling in a form, inside permission levels you set. A good agent asks a person before anything important.
Automation
Software that does a repeated job on its own, such as sending a reminder or copying details from one system to another. Automation does not need AI, and AI can make it more flexible.
CRM (customer relationship management)
The system where you keep your customers and leads, and note what happened with each one, so follow-ups do not slip.
Prompt
The instruction you give an AI. Clear instructions with examples usually get better results.
Hallucination
When an AI writes something that sounds confident but is false. It is the main reason a person should check anything important.
Human in the loop
A setup where a person approves what the AI does before it goes out or takes effect.
Training data
The material an AI learned from. A private AI for your business is set up to use your documents without sharing them with others.
RAG (retrieval-augmented generation)
A way to let an AI answer from your own documents: it looks up the right passage first, then writes the answer from it. It helps keep answers tied to what you approved.
API
A way for two programs to talk to each other, such as your website sending a form to your customer list.
Voice AI / AI receptionist
An AI that answers phone calls, takes details and passes anything urgent to a person. It should tell callers it is an AI.
Workflow
The steps a job goes through from start to finish, such as inquiry, reply, booking, reminder. Mapping the workflow is the first step in deciding what to automate.
Fine-tuning
Extra training that adapts an AI to a style or task. Many business jobs do not need it; giving the AI good instructions and your documents is often enough.
