How Key Technologies works with a business, step by step

Key Technologies · Published November 6, 2026

Short answer: we start with a free conversation, pick one small useful piece, build it around how your business already works, keep a person in charge of anything important, and only then add more.

The steps

  1. A free call. Thirty minutes about your business: what takes time, where customers or leads slip through, and what you already use. No obligation, and no sales pitch about tools you do not need.
  2. A plain-language look at where AI/SI helps and where it does not. Some jobs are a good fit, some should start as a small trial, and some should stay with a person. You get an honest answer, including “not yet.”
  3. One small piece first. Common starting points are answering missed calls and texts, following up with new inquiries, replies to reviews, or a dashboard for the numbers you check every day.
  4. Built around your business. We work from how you actually operate, using the systems you already have where that makes sense, instead of fitting you to a generic template.
  5. Permission levels you set. Each assistant is read-only, needs your approval, or runs on its own. You decide how much freedom it gets, and anything sensitive can wait for a person.
  6. Check it before relying on it. You see what it does on real examples first. AI-made content is labeled wherever a platform or the law asks for it.
  7. Add more when it earns its place. Once the first piece works, we add the next one. You can stop at any step.

What stays yours

Your accounts, customer information and systems stay in your name. Key Technologies does not host or own them. Doing the work means trusting us with real access, the same as hiring any contractor, so access is limited to what each job needs.

What we will not do

  • Promise results we cannot show, or invent numbers, clients or reviews.
  • Recommend a tool just because it is new.
  • Let an AI send something sensitive without a person approving it, unless you have chosen that.

See it first

Every service has a live demo with made-up data, so you can see how it behaves before you talk to anyone. Start with the demo library, or read our plain-English guides on what AI/SI is and how to choose an AI consultant.

Common questions

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The first call is a free, no-obligation conversation about your business and where AI/SI could help. If it is not a fit, we will say so.

Do I have to change the software I already use?

Not necessarily. We look at what you already use first. Some tools can be connected, some are better left alone, and occasionally replacing one is the better fit. That is worked out with you, not assumed.

Who owns my accounts and customer information?

You do. Your accounts, customer lists and systems stay in your name. Key Technologies does not host or own them. Doing the work does mean trusting us with real access, the same as hiring any contractor, so we limit access to what a job needs.

Will AI act on its own without my approval?

Only as much as you allow. Each assistant runs inside permission levels you set: read-only, approval required, or fully on its own. Anything sensitive can wait for a person.

Where do we start?

With one small, useful piece, such as answering missed calls, following up with new inquiries, or a dashboard for the numbers you check every day. You add more once the first piece is working.
