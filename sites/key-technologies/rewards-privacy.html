Key Rewards Privacy

Version November 25, 2026 (draft 2) · Las Vegas Restaurants

In short: we collect only what the program needs, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.
  1. Who we are. Key Rewards is a free rewards program run by Las Vegas Restaurants (@LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram) and built by Key Technologies. This page explains what information the program collects and how it is used. It is a plain-language draft and is being reviewed.
  2. What we collect. When you sign up: your name, email address and phone number. While you use the program: your rewards balance, the receipts you send (the photo or screenshot and its date, number and total), the offers you redeem, and your agreement to our terms (with the version and time). If you use the Instagram tag bonus: the link to your public post. If you join the Round One list: your first name and last initial. We do not ask for payment card numbers, passwords or government ID. Please cover any card number or personal detail on a receipt before you send the photo.
  3. How we use it. To run your account, approve your sign-up, check receipts and add rewards, let you redeem offers, prevent fraud and fake accounts, keep a permanent record of reward changes, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your information and we do not use it to show you ads.
  4. The free keys waiting list. If you join the waiting list, we collect your first and last name, Instagram handle, mobile phone, email and street address (to confirm you live in Las Vegas), your agreement to get emails and texts, and whether you ticked that you are open to trading and giving keys to friends and other members. We may also look at your public Instagram profile (only what anyone can see) to check that your account is active and that you follow @LasVegas_Restaurants. We use your information to run the list, give out keys and send you email updates and text messages about your keys. We never sell your data. It is kept private and protected, and only our team can see it. Reply STOP to a text or tap unsubscribe in an email and we stop. To be removed from the list, message @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram. Key Technologies does not buy or sell keys and never takes cash for them.
  5. Who can see it. The program owner can see your account information. A restaurant sees only what it needs when you redeem an offer there (your name and your code). The Round One leaderboard shows only your first name and last initial. Information is also processed by the services that run the program: Google Firebase (the database and website hosting), Formspree (email alerts to the owner about new sign-ups and interest forms) and Google Fonts (which loads the fonts on our pages, so your device contacts Google). Public posts you tag us in are, of course, public.
  6. How long we keep it. While the program runs and for as long as we need records of rewards and redemptions. The permanent ledger of reward changes is kept so balances can be checked; it is linked to your account code, not to your name or email. If the program ends, we may keep records for as long as the law or a dispute requires.
  7. Your choices. You can ask us to correct or delete your account information, or to stop contacting you, by sending a message to @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram. Deleting your account removes your rewards. You must be 18 or older to join.
  8. Security. We use reasonable measures to protect your information, and we are working to strengthen who can read it. No online system is perfectly secure, so please share only what the program asks for. Your account link is your login: anyone who has it can use your account, so keep it private.
  9. Cookies and tracking. We do not use advertising cookies. The pages keep a few small items on your device (your account link, your choices such as sound on or off, and queued reward records) so the app works. We count page visits without collecting your name or email.
  10. Changes to this page. We may update this page. The version date below tells you which one applies.

This page is a draft that is being reviewed. Version November 25, 2026 (draft 2). Questions or requests? Message @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram. See also the Rule Book.
