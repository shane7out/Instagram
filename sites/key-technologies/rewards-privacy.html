LVR Rewards Privacy

Version October 30, 2026 (draft 1) · Las Vegas Restaurants

In short: we collect only what the program needs, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.
  1. Who we are. LVR Rewards (Las Vegas Restaurant Rewards) is a free rewards program run by Las Vegas Restaurants (@LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram) and built by Key Technologies. This page explains what information the program collects and how it is used. It is a plain-language draft and is being reviewed.
  2. What we collect. When you sign up: your name, email address and, if you choose to give it, your phone number. While you use the program: your rewards balance, the receipts you send (the photo or screenshot and its date, number and total), the offers you redeem, your free spins and their results, and your agreement to our terms (with the version and time). If you use the Instagram tag bonus: the link to your public post. If you join the Round One list: your first name and last initial. We do not ask for payment card numbers, passwords or government ID. Please cover any card number or personal detail on a receipt before you send the photo.
  3. How we use it. To run your account, approve your sign-up, check receipts and add rewards, let you redeem offers, run the free spin and the monthly rewards share, prevent fraud and fake accounts, keep a permanent record of reward changes, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your information and we do not use it to show you ads.
  4. Who can see it. The program owner can see your account information. A restaurant sees only what it needs when you redeem an offer there (your name and your code). The Round One leaderboard shows only your first name and last initial. Information is also processed by the services that run the program: Google Firebase (the database and website hosting), Formspree (email alerts to the owner about new sign-ups and interest forms) and Google Fonts (which loads the fonts on our pages, so your device contacts Google). Public posts you tag us in are, of course, public.
  5. How long we keep it. While the program runs and for as long as we need records of rewards and redemptions. The permanent ledger of reward changes is kept so balances can be checked; it is linked to your account code, not to your name or email. If the program ends, we may keep records for as long as the law or a dispute requires.
  6. Your choices. You can ask us to correct or delete your account information, or to stop contacting you, by sending a message to @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram. Deleting your account removes your rewards. You must be 18 or older to join.
  7. Security. We use reasonable measures to protect your information, and we are working to strengthen who can read it. No online system is perfectly secure, so please share only what the program asks for. Your account link is your login: anyone who has it can use your account, so keep it private.
  8. Cookies and tracking. We do not use advertising cookies. The pages keep a few small items on your device (your account link, your choices such as sound on or off, and queued reward records) so the app works. We count page visits without collecting your name or email.
  9. Changes to this page. We may update this page. The version date below tells you which one applies.

This page is a draft that is being reviewed. Version October 30, 2026 (draft 1). Questions or requests? Message @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram. See also the Program Terms.
