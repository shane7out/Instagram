Trading watch

Every trade between two wallets, newest first, and who is trading most. Keys issued from the Treasury are not counted here. Members trade with each other; Key Technologies does not buy or sell keys.

Receipts board: who is eating where

Every receipt guests send, at every restaurant: waiting, approved and declined. Tap a photo to enlarge it.

What restaurants owe you

Real money, not keys. Every approved receipt adds your share (before tax) to what that restaurant owes you. Record each payment you get and the balance goes down. Only receipts you approve count.

Dine and earn offers

Send people to a restaurant. After they eat they send a photo of the receipt, you check it, and you give them keys. Each offer has its own link.

Send a gift to give

A gift is a credit that is not in the person's wallet. It sits on their screen as a gift card, and they can give all of it, in one piece, to a friend, a family member or anywhere they want. It can't be split.
