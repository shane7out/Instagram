FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you work with brands on Shopify, or other platforms too?

Both — most DTC automation work happens on Shopify, but the approach isn't platform-locked. What matters is your actual catalog and customer data, not which platform it sits on.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

An agency usually means an ongoing retainer for work that stays theirs — stop paying, the systems stop running. This gets built once, handed over, and is yours — with Key Technologies available for ongoing work if you want it, not because the system requires it.

Can you build the content AND the automation, or just one?

Either. Some brands want the full system — automation plus content production — others just need one piece connected to what they already have. That gets scoped in the first conversation.

Who controls our customer data?

Your customer data, your account and your systems stay in your name — Key Technologies doesn't host or own any of it. Doing the actual work does mean trusting Key Technologies with real login access, the same as hiring any contractor. In return, that work is backed by a team with 35+ years of combined security, networking and technology experience, doing everything reasonable to keep things secure. In decades of having access to client systems and databases, there's never been a client compromised.
