The Missed-Call Problem

The restaurant that answers first usually gets the booking.

During service nobody can pick up the phone, answer a direct message or reply to a review. A guest or a private-event inquiry that gets no answer usually books somewhere else. Most restaurant software tracks reservations and orders once they exist. It does not catch the call, the message or the inquiry that never became one. That gap is where real bookings get lost.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you work with a single restaurant or with groups?

Both. The system is sized to you. A single-location owner needs different automation than a group with several kitchens, and the build reflects that.

Can this work with my reservation or point-of-sale system?

It depends on the tool. Some connect directly, others are better left alone, and in some cases replacing one is the better fit. That is worked out in the first conversation, not assumed beforehand.

Will it talk to my guests without me knowing?

Only as much as you allow. Every assistant runs inside permission levels you set: read-only, approval-required, or fully on its own. Anything sensitive can wait for a person.

Who owns our accounts and guest information?

Your accounts, your guest list and your systems stay in your name. Key Technologies does not host or own them. Doing the work does mean trusting Key Technologies with real login access, the same as hiring any contractor.
