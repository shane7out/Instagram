How an event works

  1. The restaurant and Key Technologies agree the pool (rewards to share), the most one person can get, and the check-in window. The venue or restaurant agrees to take part in the event.
  2. Guests scan the event QR code and enter a first name. Each phone checks in once.
  3. Staff approve the guests who are really there and reject the rest.
  4. When you close the event, the pool is split equally among approved guests: the lesser of pool divided by guests (rounded down) and the per-person cap. Whatever is left over is never issued.
  5. Members use rewards (at least 15 each) at parties, events, meet-and-greets and participating restaurants. Staff record each use. Rewards never expire.

Rewards are whole numbers. They have no cash value, cannot be sold or exchanged for cash, and are never refunded once used.

Before this goes live

Live mode is off. This sandbox is for trying the flow. Turning it on needs, in this order:

  1. Your attorney reviews the program terms, privacy policy and the restaurant agreement (see KEY_REWARDS_PLAN.md).
  2. The owner sign-in is set up, then the staged database rules for Key Rewards are deployed.
  3. A short written deal with each restaurant.
  4. The live switch is turned on and tested with one real event with a friendly restaurant.
