Restaurants: join LVR Rewards

LVR Rewards is a free rewards promotion from Las Vegas Restaurants. Members earn rewards from their receipts and trade them for free offers at participating restaurants. If you run a restaurant and would like to be part of it, tell us a little about you below.

How it works for a restaurant

  • You choose the free offer. Every offer is worth at least $15 (15 rewards).
  • Members send in receipts, so you see real customers.
  • You show up on the Las Vegas Restaurants Instagram and in the LVR Rewards app.
