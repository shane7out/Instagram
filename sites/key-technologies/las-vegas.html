Questions

Las Vegas questions, answered

Do you work with Las Vegas businesses in person?

Yes. Key Technologies works in person around its home markets, which include Las Vegas, and online anywhere. Most of the work can be done remotely, and we meet in person where it helps.

What happens in the free review?

We talk through how your business runs today and point out where AI or automation could realistically help, and where it would not. There is no obligation and no sales pitch about tools you do not need.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We explain everything in plain language, and a person on your team stays in charge of anything customer-facing.

Which kinds of businesses do you work with in Las Vegas?

Restaurants and hospitality, medical and dental practices, law firms, real estate, e-commerce and other small and mid-sized businesses.

What does it cost?

It depends on the work, so we do not publish a price list. The first call is free, and we give you a clear quote once we understand your business.

Can you work with me in my language?

Yes. Using AI/SI, Key Technologies can communicate in virtually any language, in writing and by voice, so you can work with us in the language you are most comfortable in. For legal or medical documents, we also recommend a certified human translator.

Can I try it before I talk to anyone?

Yes. Every service has a free demo with made-up data, so you can see how it behaves before you decide anything.
