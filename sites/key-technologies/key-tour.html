Want to meet up? Optional.

You do not have to meet anyone to be part of Key Rewards or to use your keys at restaurants.

If you want to, two people can meet in person and scan each other’s code. It changes every time and works once, so it proves you were face to face, and you both earn keys. First meet-up: 20 keys each.

You can also network on Instagram, or just use your keys.

How it spreads

Group A→B→C→D→…

The first 25 people start. They are Las Vegas locals with Instagram, and we verify them. Then they share keys with Group B, C, D and so on.

The first 25 will have to trust each other. That is how friendships and a network get built.

Groups are like rooms. We can move you to another room whenever we want, for any reason, as more people enter. Getting moved is a good thing: it means the community is getting stronger. It is a game, so let’s have fun.

How the keys flow

10M→Room A→B→C→…

It starts with 10 million keys at the top. I start Room A off with 1,000 keys. A sends keys to B, B sends to C, and so on. Some people get less, some get more. That is part of the game.

As keys are used, I give more keys to Room A, and they flow down again. Group A is Room A: 25 people. Room A sends keys to Room B. Then Room B, C, D, with no end.

Use keys, move up

You can use your keys any time. Using them will not move you out of a room. It can move you up to a higher room.

Active? You move up. Not active? You move down.

We can move you to another room any time, for any reason, and being moved is a good thing. More keys can be created, but we do not plan to. We will take a community vote.
