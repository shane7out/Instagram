How the rooms work

This is a new game. There is Room 1, Room 2, Room 3, and so on, with no end. Every room holds exactly 25 people, no matter how many rooms there are. If you hear there are 80 rooms, that is 80 times 25, which is 2,000 people. Room 1 sends keys to Room 2, Room 2 sends to Room 3, and so on. We can move you to another room at any time, and being moved is a good thing: it means the community is getting stronger.

How the keys flow

It starts with 10 million keys at the top. Everyone who already holds keys is in Room 1. I give Room 1 keys, and they keep what they want and send the rest to Room 2. Room 2 keeps what it wants and sends the rest to Room 3. Room 3 keeps what it wants and sends the rest to Room 4, and so on. Some people will get less and some will get more. That is part of the game. I can also give keys directly to any room at any time, and as keys are used I give more keys to Room 1.

Use keys to move up

You can use your keys at any time. Using them will not move you out of a room. It can even move you up to a higher room: the more keys you use, the higher the rooms you can reach. If you are active, you move up. If you are not active, you move down.

How many keys are there?

Right now there are 10 million keys. The game is set up so more keys can be created, but right now we do not plan to. We will take a community vote and see where it goes. Votes are advisory.

How it spreads

The first 25 people (Room 1) start. Then they share keys with Room 2, then 3, 4 and so on. That is how we all network, build friendships and build trust. The first 25 will have to trust each other, so only trade with people you trust. We can move you from Room 1 to Room 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or any other room, whenever we want and for any reason. As more people enter the room, people are moved to other rooms. That is how the game is set up. If you get moved out of a room, take it as a good thing: it means the community is getting bigger and stronger, with more players making this happen. Please do not take offense. The rules are simple. It is a game, so let's have fun.
