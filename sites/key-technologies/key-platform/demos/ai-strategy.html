How an AI strategy review works

  1. List the work your business does and roughly how many hours each task takes.
  2. Mark how repeatable each task is and how risky it would be if it went wrong.
  3. Sort the list into good fits, tasks to pilot first and work to keep with a person.
  4. Start with one small, low-risk piece, measure the result, and only then do more.

Before it goes live

A real review looks at your actual work, not a checklist. Estimates of time saved are rough until something is running in your business, and some work should stay with people, such as decisions about credit, hiring or an upset customer.

Common questions

Will AI replace my staff?

The aim is to take repetitive work off people so they can do the work that needs judgment. Which tasks suit that depends on your business.

How much time will AI save me?

It depends on the task and on how well the system is tuned. The demo uses a rough range for illustration, not a promise.

What if AI is not the right answer?

Then we say so. Sometimes a simpler fix or a small change in process does the job.
