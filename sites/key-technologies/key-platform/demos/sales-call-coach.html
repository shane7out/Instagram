Live Sales Call Coach

Live help for sales reps and insurance agents: scripts for each kind of call, suggested next questions, answers to concerns, and AI that picks up your inbound calls.

Open Easy call mode, pick a kind of call (including final expense and life insurance), and tap or type what the person said to see a suggested next question. Nothing is sent or saved.

  • Scripts for sales calls, restaurant owners, referrals, final expense and life insurance, written to ask for a decision once and to respect a clear no

How a live call coach works

  1. Pick the kind of call (a form, a restaurant, a price question, a referral, a curious caller, final expense or life insurance) and read the script one step at a time, with the caller's name and business filled in.
  2. Before a live call, everyone is told that an AI helper is assisting and agrees. If anyone says no, the helper stays off.
  3. The screen shows the salesperson or licensed agent where they are: why the person called, the problem, what they have now, the result they want, their concerns, the ask and the next step.
  4. It suggests what to ask next from what the person just said, flags a concern as soon as it is said, and warns when the speaker is talking too much. In this demo you type or tap what was said; a live version that listens is built with you.
  5. A clear no, or a request to stop calling, ends the call. After the call it scores each step and quotes the exact words behind every score.

Before it goes live

The helper only suggests. The salesperson or licensed agent decides what to say, and it never speaks to the customer. It reads words, not tone of voice, so a suggestion is a prompt, not a verdict. Laws about listening to or recording calls differ by place, and in some places everyone must agree, so we build the notice and consent step in. Insurance sales have their own state, carrier and do-not-call rules, so the wording here is a sample and your compliance review comes before real use.

Common questions

Does the AI talk to my customer?

No. Only the salesperson or licensed agent sees the suggestions. An AI call agent, where you want one, answers your inbound calls and hands off to a person. We do not build AI that cold-calls people.

Is this for insurance agents?

Yes, the demo includes final expense and life insurance scripts for licensed agents. They are sample wording that asks for a decision once, respects a clear no, and never promises approval. Your agency and carrier rules come first.

Does everyone have to agree?

The demo asks first, because in some places everyone on a call must agree to it being recorded or analyzed. Check the rules for your location.

Will it close more sales?

We make no promise about results. It helps a salesperson or agent stay on a clear structure and see what happened on each call.

Is the demo real audio?

No. The demo does not listen. You type or tap what was said, with no microphone and no real data.
