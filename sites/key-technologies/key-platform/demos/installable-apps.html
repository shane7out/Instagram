How a home-screen app works

  1. We build the tool as a website that already works well on a phone: your dashboard, rewards card, booking page or crew check-in.
  2. We give it your name, logo and colors, plus the setup a phone needs to offer it as an app.
  3. Your customers or team open it in the phone browser and choose to add it to the home screen. It gets its own icon.
  4. From then on it opens full screen. When we improve it, everyone gets the update without installing anything again.

Before it goes live

A home-screen app is a website that behaves like an app. It does not come from an app store, and it cannot do everything a store app can. Before we build, we go through what you need it to do and say plainly whether a home-screen app is enough or a store app is the better choice.

Common questions

Do my customers need an app store account?

No. They add it from the browser, and nothing is downloaded from a store.

Is it a real app?

It installs and opens like one, with its own icon and a full-screen window. It is built with web technology, so a few phone features that store apps can use may not be available. We tell you which up front.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Both let you add a web app to the home screen, though the steps look a little different on each phone.

Does the demo install anything on my phone?

No. The phone in the demo is a picture, and everything on this page is made-up sample data.
