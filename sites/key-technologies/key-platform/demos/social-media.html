How social media management works

  1. A weekly calendar of posts is planned for the platforms you use.
  2. Captions, graphics and short video are drafted quickly with AI assistance.
  3. A person reviews and approves each post before it goes live.
  4. Content software tracks what was posted, what is scheduled next and what is performing.

Before it goes live

Volume should not cost quality. We keep a person in the approval step, label AI-made content where a platform or the law asks, and focus on posting steadily rather than posting more.
