How LVR Rewards works

  1. Dine at a participating Las Vegas restaurant and send in your receipt.
  2. After the receipt is checked, you earn 1 reward per $1.
  3. A free spin comes and goes. When it is on, you can take it for a chance at more rewards.
  4. Use your rewards for food at participating restaurants. Use them before the 1st of the month: a monthly rewards share is taken from unused rewards.

Before it goes live

Rewards have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for cash and can only be used for food at participating restaurants. Program rules can change, so the Program Terms control. This page is a demo with made-up restaurants, receipts and rewards.

Common questions

What can I use rewards for?

Food at participating restaurants only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

What is the monthly rewards share?

A share of unused rewards is taken each month, so please use your rewards before the 1st. The Program Terms have the details.

Is this demo real?

No. The restaurants, receipts and rewards in this demo are made up.
