How booking automation works

  1. The customer picks a service, a day and an open time on your website.
  2. The system checks your real calendar before telling them the time is booked.
  3. Confirmations and reminders go out by email or text on the schedule you choose.
  4. Customers can cancel or move a booking, and the time opens up again for someone else.

Before it goes live

Texts and emails need the customer's permission, and the system should never promise a time until your calendar confirms it. We set the rules for cancellations, double-booking and reminders with you first.

Common questions

Can customers book at any hour?

Yes, once it is connected to your calendar the booking page works around the clock, within the hours you allow.

Does it double-book?

It checks your calendar before confirming, which is what prevents double-booking.

Did this demo book anything?

No. Nothing here is booked, sent or saved anywhere.
