HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

Intelligent call routing.

A production blueprint for handling calls while protecting customer information and keeping the business in control.

01

Call arrives

A dedicated business number or forwarding rule sends the call to the voice provider.

→
02

AI greets caller

Receptionist identifies itself as AI and uses business-approved knowledge.

→
03

Identify intent

Questions, booking inquiries, new leads, emergencies or human handoff.

→
04

Take action

Answer, request callback, or use verified integrations to schedule or transfer.

→
05

Log outcome

Record only authorized information and notify the appropriate business staff.

SAFETY & RELIABILITY

Built with the right boundaries.

⊙

Tell callers it is AI

Use honest identification and disclosure; comply with recording and consent rules by jurisdiction.

⚿

Privacy first

Businesses are isolated from one another; role access, retention, and audit logging are required before launch.

⚑

Never invent availability

Never promise a reservation, appointment, price, or legal/medical advice without verified source data.

↪

Human fallback

Escalate uncertainty or urgent requests; support provider outage fallback and call forwarding.
