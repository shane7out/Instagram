How an AI receptionist works

  1. A call comes in on your business number, or on a line that forwards to the system.
  2. The assistant says it is an AI, greets the caller and answers common questions from information you approved, such as hours, services and location.
  3. It collects the caller's name, number and reason for calling, and can propose an appointment time.
  4. Anything urgent, unclear or sensitive is sent to a real person, and a summary lands in your inbox or CRM.

Before it goes live

Before a system like this goes live there are real decisions to make: how callers are told they are talking to an AI, whether and how calls are recorded (the rules differ by state), what your assistant may and may not say, which phone provider to use, and what a human handoff looks like after hours. Costs depend on your call volume and the provider, so they are set after we understand your business.

Common questions

Can an AI receptionist book appointments?

It can propose times and, once it is connected to your calendar, hold a slot. In this demo nothing is booked and nothing is connected to a real calendar.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It handles common questions and calls you would otherwise miss, and hands anything urgent or complicated to a person.

Is this a real phone line?

No. This is a scripted demo with a made-up business. You can play a sample call or type your own question.
