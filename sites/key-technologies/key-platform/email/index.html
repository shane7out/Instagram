EMAIL OPERATIONS

Automations with human control.

Model how emails arrive, get categorized, and become assigned business actions. Each policy can be enabled only after security and provider connection are configured.

Suggested automation rules

Low-risk drafts

Prepare answers about published hours, locations, general services and public policies. Require approval before sending by default.

High-risk escalation

Billing disputes, urgent safety issues, sensitive requests, refunds, regulated advice and uncertain answers always route to a human.

Follow-up reminders

Suggest a task when a new business inquiry has no response. Never send marketing without permission or opt-in.

After-hours handling

Acknowledge receipt only if the business explicitly enables an approved template and safe sending rules.

MULTI-BUSINESS PLATFORM

Client onboarding & permissions.

Plan how a salesperson can onboard a company while the owner retains control over account access and connected email permissions.

1. Business profile

Company details, business identity, staff roles, services, response tone, approved answers.

2. Connect a mailbox

Business owner authorizes Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 with OAuth. No password collection by KEY.

3. Choose approval mode

Start in read-and-draft mode; evaluate quality before allowing any outbound automation.

4. Test safely

Run simulated email cases, approvals, escalations, and prompt-injection tests.

5. Activate service

Enable secure message sync, scoped access, notifications, retention and supervision.

6. Measure value

Review response quality, lead handling and staff time saved using verified analytics.
