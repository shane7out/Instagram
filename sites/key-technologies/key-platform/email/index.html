How an AI email assistant works

  1. It connects to your mailbox with read-only permission first, so it can sort and label but not send.
  2. New messages are sorted by type, such as new lead, billing question or support, and flagged when they look urgent or risky.
  3. For routine messages it drafts a reply using only information you approved, such as your hours, services and policies, and shows what the draft is based on.
  4. A person reads, edits and approves the draft. Nothing goes out on its own unless you deliberately switch on a narrow, tested automation.

Before it goes live

Before connecting a real mailbox we agree what the assistant may use, which messages must always go to a person (refunds, disputes, legal, medical and anything sensitive), how approvals are logged, and how access can be turned off. Emails are treated as untrusted input, so instructions hidden inside a message are never followed.

Common questions

Will the assistant send emails by itself?

Not by default. A person approves each reply. Fully automatic replies are only for narrow cases you choose and test.

What happens to angry or sensitive emails?

They are flagged and sent to a person. The assistant does not argue with an upset customer or give advice in regulated areas.

Is this connected to a real inbox?

No. The demo uses made-up emails in your browser and sends nothing.
