How it works

  1. Join free. It takes a minute and you get your own account link.

  3. Tap Redeem on any item above. Your points come out of your balance and you get a code.
  4. Show the code to your server. That is it.

Each offer has a set number available and an end date, shown above. There is no need to hurry. Plan your visit, and please never rush or drive in a hurry to claim one.
