LVR Points

Join LVR Points. Earn free food.

Like your favorite drive-thru app, but for local Las Vegas restaurants. Join free, earn 1 point for every $1 you spend, and trade your points for free food. It is a points game: 20% of unused points go back into the game each month for other players to use. No app to download and no password: just a link that's yours.

How the monthly points share works (tap to open or close)

LVR Points is a points game, and the game works best when points keep moving. On the 1st of every month, 20% of the points a player has not used go back into the game, so other players can use them.

Example: you have 50 points on the 1st. 10 go back into the game and you keep 40. If you spend your points on a free offer before the 1st, nothing goes back.

It is not a fee and no money is involved. New players are not affected for their first 30 days.

How It Works

Join. Earn. Eat free.

STEP 1

Join free

Open your account in about 10 seconds. Then follow @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram, where new restaurants and free offers show up first.

STEP 2

Earn points

Eat at a participating restaurant, add your receipt, and earn 1 point for every $1 you spend. Your account link is your wallet: bookmark it.

STEP 3

Eat free

Trade your points for free food. Tap an offer, confirm, and show the code to your server. 20 points gets a free Chicken Bowl at Vanilla Rice's Hibachi House.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Why do I have to wait to be approved?

    We check every sign-up by hand so that only real people join LVR Points, not bots or fake accounts. After you sign up you see a "waiting for approval" page that checks by itself. As soon as you are approved, your account opens and any welcome points are added.

    How does the monthly points share work?

    LVR Points is a points game. On the 1st of every month, 20% of the points a player has not used go back into the game for other players to use. Example: you have 50 points on the 1st, 10 go back into the game and you keep 40. If you spend your points on a free offer before the 1st, nothing goes back. It is not a fee and no money is involved. New players are not affected for their first 30 days.

    Do I get points just for signing up?

    Yes. Once you are approved you start with 21 free points. After that you earn 1 point for every $1 you spend at a participating restaurant (add your receipt), and you can win more with the free spin when it is turned on.

    Is there really no password?

    Correct — your account link is your login. No username, no password to forget. Bookmark it or save it wherever you keep links you don't want to lose — if you lose it, reach out and we'll help you find your account again.

    What can I redeem points for?

    Real offers at real Las Vegas restaurants — your wallet shows everything currently available and how many points each one costs. New restaurants get added over time.

    What happens to my information?

    Your name, email and phone are used to run your account and to let the specific restaurant you redeem at see that you redeemed — it's not sold or handed to unrelated businesses. LVR Points is run by Las Vegas Restaurants, a project from the same team as Key Technologies, on its own system — separate from Key Technologies' own client work.
