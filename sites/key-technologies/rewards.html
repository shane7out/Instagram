How It Works

Three steps, no app, no password

STEP 1

Get your points

Follow @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram — that's where points drops get announced first.

STEP 2

Open your wallet

Your account link is your wallet — bookmark it. It shows your balance and everything you can redeem right now, at a glance.

STEP 3

Redeem at the restaurant

Tap an offer, confirm, and a code pops up on your phone. Show it to your server to redeem — that simple.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I get points just for signing up?

Not automatically — opening an account reserves your spot, but points get handed out to real engaged followers, mainly through @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram. Open your account first so you're ready when points go out.

Is there really no password?

Correct — your account link is your login. No username, no password to forget. Bookmark it or save it wherever you keep links you don't want to lose — if you lose it, reach out and we'll help you find your account again.

What can I redeem points for?

Real offers at real Las Vegas restaurants — your wallet shows everything currently available and how many points each one costs. New restaurants get added over time.

What happens to my information?

Your name, email and phone are used to run your account and to let the specific restaurant you redeem at see that you redeemed — it's not sold or handed to unrelated businesses. LVR Rewards is run by Las Vegas Restaurants, a project from the same team as Key Technologies, on its own system — separate from Key Technologies' own client work.
