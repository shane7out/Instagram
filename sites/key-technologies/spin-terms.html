LVR Rewards Spin Terms

Version November 3, 2026 (rev. 6) · Las Vegas Restaurants

In short: the number is random and we have no control over it.
  1. Free to join, free to spin. No purchase is necessary to join LVR Rewards or to spin. Buying food does not improve your chances.
  2. One free spin at a time. You get one free spin every 7 days. When a spin is ready you have 48 hours to use it. If you do not spin in time, that spin is gone and the wait starts again.
  3. The number is random, and we have no control over it. The winning number is picked by the random number generator built into your phone’s web browser at the moment you tap Spin. LVR Rewards, Las Vegas Restaurants, Key Technologies and the restaurants cannot choose the number, see it before you spin, or change it for anyone.
  4. The spin comes and goes. The free spin is not always turned on or available. It may be paused, changed or switched back on at any time, so keep watching for it. Nothing in the app promises that a spin will be available on any given day.
  5. Equal chances. Every number on the wheel (1 to 10) has the same chance. You receive the number you land on as free LVR Rewards.
  6. What rewards are worth. 1 reward = $1 toward a free offer at any participating restaurant, including new ones as they join. Every offer is at least 15 LVR Rewards. Rewards can only be used for those offers. They cannot be exchanged for cash, cannot be sold, and are never refunded once used.
  7. Who can spin. You must be 18 or older, and one person may have only one account. Using more than one account, or trying to interfere with the wheel, the app or the records, voids the spin and may close the account.
  8. Records. Every spin is recorded with the date, time and number, together with the version of these terms you agreed to. If you think a spin was recorded wrongly, contact us and we will review the record with you.
  9. Not gambling. The spin is a free promotion. There is nothing to bet or buy and no cash prize.
  10. Changes. We may pause, change or end the spin, or these terms, at any time, at our sole discretion. We may also cancel a spin or take back rewards won from a spin if we suspect fraud, a mistake or a security problem. Rewards have no cash value. See the Program Terms. The version date on this page tells you which terms apply.

These terms stay on this page, and under the LVR Rewards menu in the app, for as long as the spin exists. Questions? Message @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram.
