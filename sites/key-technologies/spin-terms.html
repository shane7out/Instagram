LVR Rewards Spin Terms

Version October 30, 2026 · Las Vegas Restaurants

In short: the number is random and we have no control over it.
  1. Free to join, free to spin. No purchase is necessary to join LVR Rewards or to spin. Buying food does not improve your chances.
  2. One free spin at a time. You get one free spin every 168 hours. When a spin is ready you have 48 hours to use it. If you do not spin in time, that spin is gone and the wait starts again.
  3. The number is random, and we have no control over it. The winning number is picked by the random number generator built into your phone’s web browser at the moment you tap Spin. LVR Rewards, Las Vegas Restaurants, Key Technologies and the restaurants cannot choose the number, see it before you spin, or change it for anyone.
  4. Equal chances. Every number on the wheel (1 to 10) has the same chance. You receive the number you land on as free Reward Points.
  5. What points are worth. 1 point = $1 toward a free offer at a participating restaurant. Points can also be saved for the gift cards we list on the Prizes screen, when available (for example, 1,000 points = a $200 Amazon gift card). Points cannot be exchanged for cash, cannot be sold, and are never refunded once used.
  6. Who can spin. You must be 18 or older, and one person may have only one account. Using more than one account, or trying to interfere with the wheel, the app or the records, voids the spin and may close the account.
  7. Records. Every spin is recorded with the date, time and number, together with the version of these terms you agreed to. If you think a spin was recorded wrongly, contact us and we will review the record with you.
  8. Not gambling. The spin is a free promotion. There is nothing to bet or buy and no cash prize.
  9. Changes. We may change or end the spin, or these terms, at any time. Spins already recorded are not taken back. The version date on this page tells you which terms apply.

These terms stay on this page, and under the LVR Rewards menu in the app, for as long as the spin exists. Questions? Message @LasVegas_Restaurants on Instagram.
