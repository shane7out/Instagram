FAQ

Questions, answered

What does AI/SI mean?

We started as an AI consultancy and renamed to AI/SI because AI was only ever half the story. The other half — System Intelligence — is the security, networking, databases and business infrastructure an AI system actually has to run on. A lot of businesses learn that split the hard way: an AI project that looks great in a demo and falls apart in production because nobody thought about the systems underneath it. We lead with AI/SI so that doesn't happen here — it's also why there's a free Website Audit built into this site, not just an AI pitch. And yes, it's built into our own domain: keytechnologies.si.

What does Key Technologies do?

Key Technologies helps businesses identify where AI/SI, automation and better business systems can actually improve how they operate — then builds the systems to do it.

How can AI/SI help my business?

It depends on the business. AI/SI can help with sales and lead follow-up, customer communication, repetitive operational work, organizing information, and more — the point of a Key Technologies review is figuring out which of those actually apply to you.

Do I need to know which AI/SI tools I need?

No. Most businesses don't, and that's the point of starting with the business problem instead of a specific tool. Key Technologies figures out what fits.

Can Key Technologies help small businesses?

Yes. Small businesses are often where the clearest, fastest wins are — less existing complexity to work around.

Can Key Technologies work with larger companies?

Yes. Larger organizations typically have more existing systems to map and integrate with, which the Key Technologies Method is built to handle.

What is an AI/SI agent?

An AI/SI agent is software that can carry out a defined task — like qualifying a lead, answering a common customer question, or pulling information together — rather than just responding to a single prompt.

What business processes can be automated?

Commonly: lead intake and follow-up, repetitive data entry, routine customer responses, internal reporting, and connecting tools that currently require manual work between them.

Can Key Technologies work with my existing software?

Usually, yes. The goal is to work with the systems a business already has rather than replace everything.

Can Key Technologies help with databases?

Yes — organizing, structuring and making business data easier to find and use is one of Key Technologies' core areas.

Can Key Technologies help with security and networking?

Yes. Shane's background includes high-end computer security and networking, and that's part of how AI/SI and automation get evaluated — not bolted on without regard for it.

Why did you build a free Website Audit into this site?

In October 2026, a critical unauthenticated remote-code-execution flaw in PHP itself and a separate, actively-exploited attack chain in WordPress core both made headlines, and a lot of business owners got nervous about what's actually running under their website with no easy way to check. The audit grades the things that actually determine whether a website works — speed, accessibility, best practices and SEO via Google's own PageSpeed Insights, plus that same PHP/WordPress exposure check. It's a quick, honest first read — and if it flags something, that's exactly the kind of problem Key Technologies helps fix.

How does an AI/SI consultation work?

It starts with understanding your business (the MAP step), then identifying and prioritizing real opportunities — see "How It Works" above for the full process.

What happens after I submit Explore My Options?

Shane or the Key Technologies team follows up directly to learn more about your business and talk through whether — and where — AI/SI makes sense for you.
