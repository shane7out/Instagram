Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this page covers

This describes what happens to information you give keytechnologies.si through its forms — the short and full "Explore My Options" forms, and the Free Website Audit tool. It does not cover any separate agreement you sign with Key Technologies for paid work.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site: your name, email, and (depending on the form) phone number, business name, website address, free-text answers, and any interest checkboxes you select. We don't use cookies or ad trackers.

We do log a basic page-view count for each page on this site — which page, roughly when, what page linked you here, and your screen width. No name, email, or other personal data is attached to it unless you separately submit a form. This is used only internally, to see which pages are getting looked at. The site also has Google Analytics built in but not yet turned on — once it is, this policy will say exactly what it adds.

Where it goes

Form submissions are written directly to a database Key Technologies operates, so Shane or the Key Technologies team can follow up with you. There is currently no additional login or access restriction on that database beyond normal operational use — we're telling you that plainly rather than implying stronger protection than exists today.

The Free Website Audit tool specifically

If you use the audit tool, the website address you enter is sent to two outside services to run the check:

  • Google PageSpeed Insights — to score performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO.
  • api.allorigins.win, a public proxy — used to fetch the public page source of the site you entered, so we can check it for PHP/WordPress exposure signatures client-side.

Both see the URL you submit; neither receives your name or email. We don't control either service's own data practices.

Fonts

This site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch it, the same as almost every website using a web font.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone outside Key Technologies except the two services above, only when you actively use the audit tool.

Your options

To ask what we have on file, or to have it removed, contact Shane directly through the information on this site.

Honest caveat: this is a plain-language description of what this site actually does today, not legal counsel. If you need a privacy policy for compliance purposes (GDPR, CCPA, or otherwise), have an attorney review this before relying on it.
