The Rule Book

This is the game and this is how it flows. The rules are simple. This is all new to us too, so the rules can change at any time.

1

Who this is for

Right now Key Rewards is for people who live in Las Vegas and have an Instagram account. We verify every member on Instagram before we give out keys. We are starting in Las Vegas, and we plan to open it up worldwide soon.

2

The rooms

There is Room 1, Room 2, Room 3, and so on, with no end. Every room holds exactly 20 people, no matter how many rooms there are. Your wallet card shows your room and your seat, for example Room 15, Seat 20 of 20. If you hear there are 80 rooms, that is 80 times 20, which is 1,600 people. Rooms only use numbers.

3

Room 1

Room 1 is the first 20 people, and it is full. Everyone who already holds keys is in Room 1. Room 2 is open now.

4

The keys

The game starts with 10 million keys. The game is set up so more keys can be created, but right now we do not plan to. We will take a community vote and see where it goes. Votes are advisory. I am not keeping any of the starting keys for myself.

5

How the keys flow

I give Room 1 keys. Each person in Room 1 can choose to send a percentage of their wallet to Room 2, and it is shared evenly among everyone in Room 2. Each person in Room 2 can do the same for Room 3, Room 3 for Room 4, and so on. You choose the percentage, and you do not have to send any. When you open your wallet, it tells you which room you are in. I can also give keys directly to any room at any time. As keys are used, I give more keys to Room 1.

6

Using your keys

You can use your keys at any time: restaurants, gift cards, trading, experiences and more. Using them will not move you out of a room. It can move you up to a higher room. The more keys you use, the higher the rooms you can reach. If you are active, you move up. If you are not active, you move down.

7

Moving rooms

We can move you to any other room, whenever we want and for any reason. As more people enter, people are moved to other rooms. If you get moved, take it as a good thing: it means the community is getting bigger and stronger. Please do not take offense.

8

Keys expire

Keys are time sensitive. They are good for 60 days from the day they are issued, and keys you do not use disappear. A gift card for a friend or family member is good for 14 days. If you give keys to someone, they keep the same expiry date.

9

Meeting up is optional

You do not have to meet anyone to be part of Key Rewards or to use your keys at restaurants. If you want to, two people can meet in person and scan each other’s one-time code. The code changes every time and works once. You both earn keys: 20 each for your first meet-up. You can only earn from meeting the same person once.

10

Trading and giving

Key Rewards is a game of trust: people trust each other, build relationships and make friendships, and the keys bring everybody together. Trading and giving keys to friends and family is optional. Only trade with people you trust. Never send or accept keys for money. We do not buy or sell keys, we never take cash, and we have no control over what members do between themselves.

11

Fair play

Everybody has to trust each other. If you see any sign of foul play, fraud or misuse, please tell us. You may be rewarded with more keys. We review every report. False reports do not earn keys.

12

Open source

The Key Rewards code is going to be open source, so everybody can see the rules and the code. There is nothing hidden.

13

The marketplace

Members can list their stuff, buy stuff and trade stuff. Think of it as a bartering system, paid in keys. Only members who hold keys can post on the marketplace. Post what you like and keep it clean. Anyone who posts something bad can be banned and lose all their keys. We may give keys for posting.

14

Votes and ideas

Keys can be used to vote on community ideas. Members with more keys get more votes, including me. Votes are advisory. Send us your ideas and comments. We read every one.

15

Giveaways

We are doing big giveaways. Our goal is to give away houses, cars and cash. These are goals, not promises yet. When we give away real prizes, the winners are picked with random number generator software, so it is fair.

16

Your information

We send you email updates and text messages about your keys. Reply STOP to a text or tap unsubscribe in an email and we stop. We use your address to confirm you live in Las Vegas. We never sell your data, and we keep it private and protected.

17

Scam warning

We will never ask you for money, cash, credit cards or any payment. Never. If anyone contacts you asking for money, it is a scam.

18

The game can change

This is a game, and it is all new to us too. We can change the rules at any time. We can change the game at any time. We can take keys away, ban anyone, move anyone and close the game at any time, for any reason. We will do our best to keep it going and build it out for the community.

19

Who built it

I built this software for the community, after 10 years of dedication to this channel. Key Technologies built the software and is not responsible for how keys are used by members or in the future. Keys have no cash value. Keys are not shares, ownership or an investment. This is not sponsored or run by Instagram.
