California Privacy Rights

Last updated: January 2025

This section applies to California residents and supplements our Privacy Policy. It describes your rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended.

Your rights

Subject to certain limitations, California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion of their personal information, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of their personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

To exercise your right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, you may use the request below. In a live deployment this control would submit your opt-out preference; on this demonstration page it does not transmit any data.

How to submit a request

You may also submit a rights request through the contact information provided on our website. We will verify your request before responding, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
