Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how SparkyLoan (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and loan matching service. By using the service, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

SparkyLoan is not a lender. We operate a matching service that connects consumers with a network of third-party lenders and lending partners. We do not make credit decisions or fund loans ourselves.

Information we collect

To match you with lenders, we may collect the information you provide in our request form, such as your name, email address, phone number, requested loan amount, and loan purpose. We may also collect technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages viewed.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to search our lender network for options that may fit your situation, to operate and improve our service, to communicate with you, and to comply with applicable law.

How we share your information

When you submit a request, we may share the information you provide with lenders and lending partners in our network so they can evaluate whether they can offer you a loan. We may also share information with service providers who support our operations, and as required by law.

Your choices

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information. California residents can review our California privacy disclosures.

Data security

Your data is protected with state-of-the-art encryption, and we take reasonable measures to safeguard the information we collect. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the information provided on our website.
